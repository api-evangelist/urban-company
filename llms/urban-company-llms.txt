# Urban Company

> Urban Company (formerly UrbanClap) is a home services marketplace that connects consumers with trained, background-verified service professionals for home services, appliance and electronics repair, beauty and salon-at-home services, cleaning, plumbing, electrical, and carpentry work. It operates across cities in India as well as international markets including the UAE and Saudi Arabia, and serves customers through its website and iOS/Android apps. Service professionals join and manage work through a dedicated partner platform. Urban Company does not publish a public developer API program; its engineering team maintains an open-source GitHub organization and an engineering blog.
